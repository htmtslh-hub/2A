What we run

Three programmes,
one shared record

Sponsorship funds all three. You choose where yours goes, and you can move it at any time.

Mapping

Divers photograph fixed transects every six weeks. The images become a coral-cover figure anyone can check against last season's.

26 sites on the schedule

Replanting

Fragments grown on rope nurseries go back onto bare rock. Survival is counted at six and twelve months, and the failures are published too.

71% alive after twelve months

Local crews

Every dive team is hired and trained from the nearest village. Nine of our twelve site leads started as boat crew.
