# Paddle domain appeal draft

Send only after the updated storefront is deployed and its claims match the
actual products and fulfillment. This draft has not been submitted.

Subject: Request for re-review of forgezone.store

Hello Paddle Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing forgezone.store. I would like to request a re-review
and clarify the products intended for sale through Paddle.

The storefront offers ready-made downloadable website templates. Customers
purchase a template license, receive access to its source files through their
account, and follow the included installation and customization guidance.

We found that legacy service pages and the former dichvu.forgezone.store
subdomain could still be reached during the earlier reviews. Those pages and
service enquiry endpoints have now been retired and return HTTP 410. There is
no service offering or service checkout on forgezone.store or its subdomains.

The live catalogue now shows only three products that have real downloadable
packages: Kinetiq, Tidal and Dune Pass. Each ZIP contains an HTML/CSS/JavaScript
website, README, editing guide and licence. Payment automatically unlocks the
download in the customer's account. The displayed price does not include
custom design, development, consulting or installation.

Could you perform a manual re-review of the updated storefront and confirm
whether these downloadable products are eligible? We can provide a sample ZIP,
proof of authorship, and a demonstration of the automated delivery flow.

Thank you,
Dinh Van

## Evidence to prepare

- A working template demo and its corresponding downloadable package.
- Evidence of authorship or redistribution rights for every template sold.
- Screenshots of product pricing, license, refund policy and account downloads.
- A verified payment-to-download flow using the approved test environment.
- Confirmation that Paddle products do not include custom development,
  installation labor or consulting fees.
