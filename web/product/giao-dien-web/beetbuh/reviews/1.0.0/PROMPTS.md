# Image generation direction

Reference: user screenshot, treated as visual data. Three separate transparent PNG requests using the built-in ImageGen tool.

1. Product hero: isolated premium orange sculptural over-ear headset, upright three-quarter pose, tall curved arch, organic open lattice shell, orange fabric cushions, dark circular earcup cap with tiny red light, no typography or environment.
2. Product angle: same orange lattice headphone design, open angled composition showing both plush earcups and arch, transparent background, full silhouette with generous square margins, matching material/colour/light.
3. Lifestyle: fictional young Black male campaign model with short natural twists, eyes closed, face tilted up to the right, orange hoodie and matching organic orange headphones, chain, waist-up transparent cutout, soft studio lighting; no real-person identity or endorsement.

Generated assets were inspected individually before conversion to WebP. They reconstruct the reference subject/pose; fine lattice geometry, facial identity and fabric detail differ. They are not original manufacturer renders.
