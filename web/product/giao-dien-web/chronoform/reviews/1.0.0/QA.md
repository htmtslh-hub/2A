## Older generic checker resolutions

The eight reported failures remain visible in checks.json. Menu resetOnDesktop assumes the toggle disappears on desktop; this video-led design keeps it present. Actual resize closes the menu and Escape restores focus at every width. The other six allVisible failures count hidden inactive-route reveals and below-fold content that was not scrolled. The newer 05/10 motion policy requires motion ON under OS reduce, so forcing all reveals visible on load would contradict the request. fallbacks.json actually scrolls the active collection in file/offline/blocked-WebGL modes and verifies opacity 1 and working detail; four-engine tests measure motion under OS reduce and stale-off. No shared checker or rules were weakened.

## Interaction limits

Session bag/favourites reset on reload. The sample bag contains orange and ice Opus models, total €28,400 with illustrative VAT arithmetic. EUR/USD are fixed sample amounts, not exchange rates.
