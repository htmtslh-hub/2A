02 / Macro & Ingredient Architecture

Whole Ingredients Measured to the Gram

Our club kitchen preps small batches every morning at 05:30. Expand any category below to inspect ingredient sourcing, preparation methods, and allergen notes.

01 · Hydro-Cooled Greens & Citrus Base +

Green oakleaf lettuce, Persian cucumber ribbons, vine-ripened cherry tomatoes, and sweet basil leaves washed in triple-filtered chilled water and dressed to order.

  • Micronutrients: Vitamin K, Vitamin C, folate, and natural potassium
  • Dressing: Cold-pressed Valencia orange & extra-virgin olive oil emulsion
02 · Slow-Roasted Chickpeas, Walnuts & Activated Granola +

Garbanzo beans roasted at 165°C with sea salt and sweet paprika, paired with raw walnut halves, whole almonds, pumpkin seeds, and oat-maple clusters.

  • Allergen note: Contains tree nuts (walnuts, almonds) and gluten-free rolled oats
  • Texture profile: Served in a separate ceramic side bowl to preserve crunch
03 · Polyphenol Berries & Haas Avocado Fats +

Blackberries, blueberries, strawberries, and pomegranate seeds supply natural anthocyanins alongside monounsaturated fats from fresh Haas avocado.

  • Glycemic balance: Paired with fibre and healthy fats for steady energy release
