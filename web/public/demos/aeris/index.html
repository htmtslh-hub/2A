01 — The sound

Engineered around the way you listen.

  1. 01

    Sound with room to breathe.

    40 mm dynamic drivers keep bass full and controlled while vocals and strings stay clear, even at low volume.

  2. 02

    Quiet, whenever you need it.

    Hybrid noise cancelling uses six microphones to soften engines, traffic and air conditioning. One press switches to ambient mode.

  3. 03

    A battery that keeps up.

    Up to 40 hours with noise cancelling off, 30 hours with it on. Ten minutes over USB-C adds about four hours.

02 — Comfort by design

Soft where it touches. Strong where it folds.

Every surface that meets your head is cushioned. Every part that moves is reinforced. The result disappears after the first song.

  • 01

    Memory-foam cushions

    Slow-recovery foam settles around your ears and spreads pressure evenly.

  • 02

    Fold-flat steel hinges

    Ear cups rotate flat and tuck inward for the included soft case.

  • 03

    238 g, all-day light

    A lighter frame and a wide headband keep long sessions comfortable.
