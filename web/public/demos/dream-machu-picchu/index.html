FIVE REASONS WHY
YOU SHOULD VISIT
MACHUPICCHU

The Inca Trail. This is one of the most popular ways to get to Machu Picchu, hiking over a long distance in the Andes Mountains, camping on the trail, and being able to see other Incan ruins.
The amazing view. Even in pictures, seeing the citadel from the Sun Gate does not compare to standing above the Urubamba cloud forest in person.
The Mystery. Built by the Incas centuries ago high in the Peruvian Andes, the sacred citadel remained hidden above the Urubamba gorge until 1911.
Other sites en route. Patallacta and Wiñay Wayna are monumental terraced sanctuaries you pass along the classic high-mountain Inca Trail.
Being an explorer. Cutting your way through cloud forests, exploring ancient temples, and crossing hand-woven Incan suspension bridges.
