Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Thin-film interference across nanometer-scale coatings shifts specular reflections from emerald to violet as the viewing angle changes.
Index of refraction — The ratio of light velocity in vacuum to the medium bends rays entering and exiting each beveled shard face.
Dispersion — Wavelength-dependent refraction separates white studio light into spectral chromatic rims along high-curvature edges.
Transmission — Physically based attenuation and background sampling preserve underlying typography while tinting transmitted light.
Roughness — Microfacet distribution scatters highlights across frosted smoked-glass faces while keeping clearcoat rims razor-sharp.
