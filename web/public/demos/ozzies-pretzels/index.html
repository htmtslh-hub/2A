HOW IT'S MADE — THE OZZIE'S WAY

24-Hour Cold Ferment, 12-Minute Stone Bake

STEP 01

Colorado Flour

Unbleached malted winter wheat mixed with pure Rocky Mountain spring water.

STEP 02

Hand-Twisted

Rolled to a 28-inch tapered rope and snapped in mid-air for thick belly & crispy arms.

STEP 03

Hearth Baked

Scored on the belly and baked on deck stones for signature mahogany Maillard snap.

STEP 04

Courier Express

Dispatched across Denver in insulated linen pouches within 35 minutes of baking.
